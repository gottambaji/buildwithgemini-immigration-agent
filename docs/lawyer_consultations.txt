# Immigration Lawyer Directory & Consultation Advisory Services

## Immigration Attorney Referral Network
VisaSphere connects non-immigrant visa holders (H-1B, F-1 OPT, L-1, O-1, Green Card applicants) with vetted, licensed AILA (American Immigration Lawyers Association) attorneys for legal consultations.

## Services Offered by Partner Immigration Lawyers:
1. 221(g) Administrative Processing & Refusal Response: Direct filing of supplemental legal memoranda to US consulates.
2. Request for Evidence (RFE) & NOID Defense: Preparing complex USCIS RFE responses for H-1B petitions, PERM labor certifications, and I-140 filings.
3. Employer Transition & Layoff Counsel: Emergency legal advice within the 60-day H-1B grace period, including change of status to B-1/B-2, O-1, or dependent H-4 status.
4. PERM & I-140 Priority Date Strategy: EB-2 / EB-3 NIW (National Interest Waiver) evaluations for qualifying professionals.

## How to Book an Attorney Consultation:
Users facing complex legal issues (e.g., visa refusal, lay-off grace period, 221g yellow slip) can submit a consultation request through VisaSphere to be matched with an experienced immigration attorney.
